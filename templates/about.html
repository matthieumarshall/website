About Us

The Oxfordshire Cross Country League is run by volunteers and exists to deliver safe, competitive cross country fixtures for local clubs.

Our mission

We aim to provide a welcoming league calendar, clear entry guidance, and accurate results for athletes, team managers, and clubs across Oxfordshire.

What we do

  • Organise league fixtures and publish race results.
  • Maintain entry processes for club managers.
  • Support fair competition through transparent rules and standings.
